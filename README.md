# GreenEdge Landscaping — Business Analytics & BI Case Study

## Project Overview

GreenEdge Landscaping is a fictional landscaping and field-service business created to demonstrate an end-to-end business analytics and reporting workflow.

The project transforms raw operational and financial data into validated business reporting, profitability analysis, operational insights, customer analysis, payment analysis, and an executive dashboard.

## Dataset

- 82,004 jobs
- 1,800 customers
- 47,004 payments
- 10 services
- 10 crews
- Data period: January 2023 – August 2026

## Analysis Performed

### Data Quality & Validation
- Data cleaning and standardization using Power Query
- Duplicate and completeness checks
- Financial reconciliation
- Cross-table validation
- Data quality review

### Business Analysis
- Revenue and job analysis
- Service profitability and gross margin
- Labor and operational analysis
- Customer activity and revenue concentration
- Payment status and outstanding-payment analysis
- KPI development

### Dashboard

The project includes an executive dashboard showing:

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin
- Total Jobs
- Annual Revenue Trend
- Gross Profit by Service
- Payment Status
- Customer Activity
- Operational Metrics

## Key Results

- Total recorded revenue: **$38.08M**
- Gross profit: **$25.24M**
- Gross margin: **66.3%**
- Total jobs: **82,004**
- Customers: **1,800**
- Recorded labor hours: **272,636**
- Jobs with recorded hours: **99.4%**

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- PivotTables
- Excel formulas
- Data validation
- Business intelligence reporting
- Dashboard development

## Business Value

This project demonstrates how messy operational data can be transformed into a structured reporting system that helps a business understand:

- Where revenue is coming from
- Which services contribute to profitability
- Customer activity and concentration
- Payment exposure
- Operational performance
- Data-quality issues that may affect reporting

- Excel business analysis workbook
- Executive dashboard

- Profitability analysis
- Customer analysis
- Payment analysis

## Note

GreenEdge Landscaping is a fictional business created for portfolio and demonstration purposes. The project is intended to demonstrate the analytics methodology and reporting capabilities rather than represent a real client engagement.
